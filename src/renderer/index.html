Model scope & interpretation

Bounded dry slope preparation can move bed material before initial water filling. Its passes are not years or climate-driven erosion. Water, drainage, and basins then use the prepared bed.

Manual runoff can fill and spill between unambiguous basins. It has no elapsed time, climate forcing, or changing drainage; unsupported routes are rejected. Save recipe returns to initial water; a water checkpoint saves this manual inventory separately.

Temperature maps show repeatable monthly normals from a prescribed solar-response model on initial geography. They are not weather, energy-balanced climate, or a changing water cycle.

Land and water shows a separate water surface on the globe, limited to displayed wet regions and occluded by the interpolated bed. Its shoreline and levels are visual approximations; exact basin stocks remain in the native core. Depth and geology layers show the bed. Land colors are elevation-based, not biomes.

Rivers, precipitation, and ongoing erosion are not implemented. Diagnostic playback does not change terrain, temperature, or water.
