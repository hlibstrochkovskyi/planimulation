WHAT THIS MODEL MEANS

Initial water depth follows the geological bed and one fitted level. The largest connected water body is called the main ocean; others are inland basins. This does not model salinity.

Land and water shows a separate water surface on the globe, limited to wet regions and occluded by the interpolated bed. Its visible shoreline is approximate; regional statistics remain authoritative. Depth and geology layers show the bed. Land colors are elevation-based, not biomes.

Erosion, rivers, and climate are not implemented. Diagnostic playback does not change terrain or water.
