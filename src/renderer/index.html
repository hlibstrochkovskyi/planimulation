Model scope & interpretation

Bounded dry slope preparation can move bed material before initial water filling. Its passes are not years or climate-driven erosion. Water, drainage, and basins then use the prepared bed.

Manual runoff can fill and spill between unambiguous basins. It has no elapsed time, climate forcing, or changing drainage; unsupported routes are rejected. Save recipe returns to initial water; a water checkpoint saves this manual inventory separately.

Seasonal water is a separate finite six-stock experiment driven by prescribed monthly temperature and wind. It starts with a dry atmosphere and up to a 1 m mobile layer of initial water. Snow, soil, delayed runoff, terminal retention, evaporation, and precipitation evolve in the native core. No deep-water replenishment, groundwater, changing coast, lake spill levels, or calibrated climate is implied.

Temperature and wind maps inspect repeatable monthly normals independently of the seasonal-water clock; neither layer is weather. Regional surface flow and unified soil water show evolving terrestrial water caps plus prescribed initial reference-body heads in Land and water; other seasonal modes retain initial or manual geometry. Pool columns use fixed region area and are not resolved lake depths. Land colors are elevation-based, not biomes.

Unified soil water is a separate five-owner family: terrestrial liquid, soil, snow, vapor and delayed soil drainage, plus one finite stock per initial reference body. Terrestrial soil remains active under liquid films. Exact received high/low components are visible in the inspector. Its explicit retainDonor numerical policy leaves unresolved transfers at their donors; deferred-request diagnostics are neither completed flow nor lost water. Complete schema-1 regional-seasonal-water saves are distinct from unsupported legacy schema 1. No old file is converted. The older regional surface-flow mode retains schema 15 and its original behavior.

Seasonal display frames are not checkpoints. Save seasonal checkpoint preserves the complete schema-3 baseline, schema-4 experimental upslope, or schema-7 precise upslope state; Open seasonal checkpoint validates a separate candidate before replacing the accepted world. Only pinned default climate settings are supported. Precise upslope retains low components of liquid, snow, soil, and terminal mass; it changes numerical representation, not the empirical climate laws. Upslope response accelerates removal of existing supersaturation; it is not a cloud or vertical atmospheric solver. Select a mode before initialization; regenerate to switch. Recipes and manual water checkpoints do not save seasonal progress. Unsaved progress is discarded on regeneration or application reload. Camera and last-interval rate displays are not saved. Rivers with channel hydraulics, weather, and ongoing erosion remain future work. Diagnostic playback is separate graph diffusion.
